# Tabular Evidence Capability

LuoHai owns XLSX observation, table-region detection, worksheet/table context,
cell and range evidence, declared-table metadata comparison, backend loss
declarations, and citation reconstruction. Ambiguous context or multiple
matches produce explicit ambiguity rather than a first-match choice.

LuoHai does not ingest legal text, establish authority, perform cross-Skill
ranking, or provide application orchestration. Those concerns remain
ShanHai-owned, unsupported, or application-owned.
